# evidence

Two pictures, kept because a rule in `src/styles/tokens.css` is only a
sentence without them.

| | |
|---|---|
| `rare-wide-bands.webp` | Rare's two sweeps at 22% and 21% of the gradient. mean 20.93 — **above** Epic's 20.61 |
| `rare-narrow-bands.webp` | the same peaks at 16% and 15%. mean 15.99, Epic 29% clear of it |

Both are the same card, the same player, the same pinned light, with only the
`r-*` class swapped: C, R, E, L left to right.

**What they are evidence of.** A lower step was reading brighter than a higher
one, and there were two ways to fix it. Dropping the peaks was the obvious
one and it is the wrong one:

> Contrast is what makes the step legible; area is what makes the mean.
> Dimming spends both. Narrowing spends only the second.

Rare is achromatic *because* brightness is the one axis every illustration
supplies for free — so a fix that spends brightness would have taken away the
reason this step exists. **A fix that undermines the case for the thing it is
fixing is not merely worse; it is self-contradicting.**

Put side by side, the bands narrow and stay just as legible, and the only
thing that changes is the number that was wrong. That is the whole argument,
and it cannot be made in prose.

Regenerate either with `tools/holo/ladder.mjs` after editing `.r-R`.
